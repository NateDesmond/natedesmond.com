Projects

No. 01
2Platforms, iOS and Android

Wordbrush

Chinese vocabulary, built for an audience of one

I kept forgetting the same characters, so I made the tool I wanted. Wordbrush teaches words in frequency order, which means the effort lands on the vocabulary you actually meet. Every entry carries native audio. Spaced repetition schedules the reviews so you do not have to.

No streak shame, no currency to buy. Just the words, in the order that pays.

No. 02
45Episodes, one season

Horizons Pod

A marketing podcast that went behind the scenes

One season, about 45 conversations with the operators who grew companies like Uber, Masterclass, Flipkart, Shopify, and Bumble. The interesting part was never the headline number. It was the experiment that failed first, and what they built instead.

When the season ended, I turned all 45 episodes into songs. It made sense at the time. Listen to the podcast.

No. 03
10Seasons, all free in the browser

Butterfell

A cozy creamery sim with a business school underneath

You run a small pixel-art creamery. You churn, you stock, you price, you try to end the season with cash in the drawer. What the game never tells you is that it is walking you through an operations core: the newsvendor problem when you guess the order wrong, working capital when growth outruns the bank, the bullwhip effect when everyone upstream overcorrects.

Nobody is tested. The lessons just arrive. Play at butterfell.urbanalgorithm.com.
